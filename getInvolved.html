Get involved

AI Water Usage is a student-led effort to bring transparency to one of the most overlooked environmental impacts of artificial intelligence.

This project started as a school poster war and grew into a real-time, research-backed resource for understanding the true cost of AI.

As AI spreads, so does the hidden cost of operating the data centers that power it. Cooling systems — especially water-based evaporative cooling — consume enormous amounts of fresh water, and most people have no idea it is happening.

Our goal is not to scare anyone away from AI, but to encourage responsible development and use through awareness. Numbers only change behavior when people can actually see them.

What you can do

Five ways to help, from the easiest thing you can do today to building alongside us. Step through them.

Step 1 of 5

Talk about it

Mention AI's water footprint in your classes, clubs, or conversations. Most people haven't heard about it until someone tells them.

Step 2 of 5

Share the site

Post a link to aiwaterusage.com on social media, pin it in a Discord, or put a QR code on a bulletin board. On a phone, tap Share and choose Instagram to post it straight to your story.

Step 3 of 5

Ask questions

When you use or build with AI, ask how much energy and water it takes, and what is actually powering the backend.

Step 4 of 5

Build something

A poster, a podcast, a school talk, or your own research — find a way to add to the conversation.

Step 5 of 5

Collaborate with us

Want to help gather data, design charts, or build new features? Email contact@aiwaterusage.com — we welcome new ideas and new contributors.

Small actions lead to big changes.

If you've read this far, you're already doing your part — and we appreciate you. Questions or ideas? Email contact@aiwaterusage.com.
