AI Water Usage is a student-led effort to bring transparency to one of the most overlooked environmental impacts of artificial intelligence.

This project started as a school poster war and grew into something bigger: a real-time, research-backed resource for anyone who wants to understand the true cost of AI.

As AI becomes more widespread, so too does the hidden cost of operating the massive data centers that power it. Cooling systems — particularly water-based evaporative cooling — consume enormous amounts of fresh water. Most people have no idea it's happening.

Our Mission

We aim to demystify how much water AI actually uses. Our goal is not to scare people away from AI, but to encourage responsible development and usage through awareness. Numbers only change behavior when people can actually see them.
