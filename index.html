Methodology

How these numbers work

What the counters show
Each provider's figure is a running total, built up from its known daily or annual water rate since April 10, 2025. These are calculated accumulations, not live measurements from the data centers.
Reported versus estimated
Reported figures come from a company's own environmental reporting, such as Google's 2023 report. Estimates are derived from partial disclosures, journalism, and peer-reviewed research, and carry more uncertainty.
Why the figures rise
The total adds each provider's per-second share of its annual rate every second the page is open. It reflects elapsed time against a fixed rate, so it climbs steadily rather than reacting to real demand.
Where infrastructure overlaps
OpenAI runs on Microsoft Azure and Anthropic runs on Amazon's AWS. Their water is already inside Microsoft's and Amazon's numbers, so the combined total counts only the four independent providers.
Outlook
Research by Li et al. (2023) projects global AI water use of 4.2–6.6 trillion liters a year by 2027. At that midpoint rate, about — would be drawn worldwide so far this year. This is a projection, shown separately from the provider counters above.
Units
Figures are shown in US gallons by default; the toggle converts to liters at 3.785 liters per gallon. Everyday comparisons use 17 gallons per eight-minute shower, 19 per laundry load, and 660,000 per Olympic pool.

Every figure here is drawn from public research and corporate reporting. The full, cited source list is on the Sources page.
